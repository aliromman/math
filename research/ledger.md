## Earlier work

PRs 1–4 and 7 document a tree-matching counterexample, three Walker
inequalities, and the restricted U-polynomial construction. In the latter
investigation, cutting the joining edge distinguished the exchanged
branches. Keeping that edge led to a pair with identical low-order cut
data. The unrestricted tree problem distinguished the pair at a higher
order. The two partial investigations are Stanley's tree isomorphism
question (PR 6) and the convex heptagon threshold (PR 9). Their finite
searches do not resolve the general questions. PR 5 concerns research policy.

## Investigations outside the result count

| Investigation | Evidence and remaining question |
| --- | --- |
| Braun–Develin and Ferroni–Higashitani | Pfeifle's angle bounds already imply the first proposed conclusion. Published counterexamples to the second appear in arXiv:2609.10513. |
| Hillion–Johnson and Kovačević | An entropy monotonicity search tested 22,000 vectors with 2–30 Bernoulli variables at eight orders. For maximum entropy of sums, optimization from 32 starts in each of 15 cases reached the conjectured value within rounding. Both global arguments remain open. |
| Dujella, Pell-system parameters and complex quintuples | Vieta descent tested 2,705,418 noninitial nodes through k ≤ 9 × 10¹⁸. Only the known repeated maximum 33,539 appeared. A separate mixed-sign rational-integer search excluded 919,901,012 candidates with r ≤ 10⁶. Its restriction leaves the complex problem open. |
| Erdős–Herzog–Piranian, polygon distance product | The Hessian at the regular odd polygon was negative in the nonrigid modes. This gives local evidence. A global bound remains open. |
| Batta–Hajdu–Pongrácz, Diophantine graphs | The search found no induced pyramid among 11,900,060 Vieta triangles with hub ≤ 10,000 and other roots ≤ 2 × 10¹¹. A separate search over 34,259,264 incidences found no specified K₃,₃ with first vertex ≤ 1,000, remaining vertices ≤ 10⁶ and first incident roots ≤ 200,000. |
| Ruderman–Erdős, Egyptian fractions | A large denominator can cover several prime divisors at once. This prevents the proposed denominator count from proving the lower bound. |
| Sagan–Swanson | A published sum-of-products proof already gives the bottom-heavy inequality. Two exact scans of the stronger type-B parity log-concavity question found no violation through n = 95. |
| Fan–Sun, loneliness spectrum | For each runner count 7–85, an exact search replaced one speed in the standard progression by two speeds at most ten times the count. It found no violation in this family. |
| Bárány–Füredi, lattice diameter | Tests with offsets at most five from square corners found no counterexample in quadrilaterals for k + 1 from 6 to 44, or centrally symmetric hexagons for k + 1 from 6 to 38. |
| Pálvölgyi, necklace division | A four-recipient heuristic found no failure in 20,000 three-color inputs of six beads per color, 1,800 of ten beads per color, or 2,800 four-color inputs of six beads per color. |
| Blitvić–Elvey Price–Troyka and Berger–Mani | For the matching pattern 121323, the first 17 Stieltjes coefficients were positive. This finite prefix leaves the moment question open. A proposed Fourier bound for sum-free sets already fails at {1,3,4} with eigenvalue −5/9. |
| Somu–Tran and Erdős–Purdy–Straus | The second campaign deferred a polygonal construction until its initial interval could be certified, and an area candidate until all triangle constraints and a published bound could be checked. The later studies are PRs 73 and 81. Each contributes once to the third campaign. |
| Rahmann–Rivals, missing-word variance | Prior work gives the leading term for fixed word length and growing alphabet. A conditioning argument suggested a sharper endpoint error. The exact source claim and error bound remain unverified. |
| Breen–Kempton–Knudson–Shumway, nonbacktracking walks | A proposed fixed-target comparison uses a positive semidefinite form on directed edges and strictness at a neighbor of the target. It still needs independent review and formalization. |
| Grid pebbling | Four pebbles per site of an index-14 lattice recover the known covering ratio 7/2. An acyclic flow restricted to radius seven with edge flows at most three failed to reach the missing residue of a different placement. The unrestricted conjecture remains open. |
| Triangular-lattice distances and Penney races | A radius-squared-315 disk improves the corresponding regular hexagon in the finite comparison. Earlier work by Balaji and colleagues tabulates stronger instances. A previously published Penney-race limit also excluded a second independent derivation. |
| Exclusions for prior work | Prior results cover the Cameron–Dey group question (Lazorec), Alikhani's integral total domination roots, Gawron's rational-root density question (Schinzel), and the hidden-forest reconstruction (Kolossváry). Later screening also found prior resolutions for Nathanson's lattice diameter problem, Fang's arcsine question, Ho's expansion question, the Pula–Song–Wanless threshold, and Brown–McMullin's reliability-root density. |
